⚠️ Before you ride or walk

Every route in this app is generated automatically by a computer from open map data. No person has ridden, walked or checked it.

  • Map data can be wrong or out of date. A route may use a road that is busy, fast, closed, under construction, private, unlit or unsuitable for you or your bike, or a path that no longer exists.
  • Safety, traffic and other ratings are estimates from incomplete data, not guarantees. A "low stress" or "safe" rating does not mean a route is safe.
  • Weather, road works and hazard reports may be missing, late or wrong.
  • Always watch the road, obey signs and traffic laws, and use your own judgement. If a route looks unsafe, don't take it.
  • Don't look at or handle your phone while moving: stop somewhere safe first. Use lights after dark and suitable safety gear, and carry what you need.

Use the routes, directions and voice guidance at your own risk. Do not rely on this app for safe navigation.
